Questions people ask

Straight answers, no fine print.

Is PowerFold Theory free?

The first week is all access with no card. After that, Lessons 1 and 2, five practice hands a day and one Ask Ace question a day stay free for good. Premium opens everything for $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.

Do I need to know poker already?

No. Lesson 1 starts with how the table works and the words you will hear. If you already play, skip to The Table and let the feedback find your leaks.

Is this gambling?

No. There is no real money, no betting against other people and no ads. PowerFold Theory is a trainer: one decision at a time, then honest feedback on the better play and why.

Why does it hide whether I would have won?

Because results lie. A good fold that would have hit the river is still a good fold, and a bad call that got lucky is still a bad call. Hiding the outcome keeps you judging the decision, which is the only thing you control.

Who is Ace?

Ace is the app's AI poker coach, a patient cowboy mentor built on Anthropic's Claude. Ask him anything about poker, as many times as you need. Your questions are not stored.

Does it work offline?

The lessons live on your phone and work without a signal. Practice hands and Ask Ace need a connection.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. PowerFold Theory is on Android today. Join the waitlist below and you will get one email when it reaches the App Store.
